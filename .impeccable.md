## Design Context

### Users
Movie and TV enthusiasts use CineTrekker on Android to find worthwhile titles,
keep an intentional watchlist, remember what they have watched, and keep that
progress available across devices.

### Brand Personality
Focused, cinematic, and quietly confident. CineTrekker should feel like a
well-curated companion for deciding what to watch, not a noisy streaming
catalogue or a gamified feed.

### Aesthetic Direction
The mobile website at cinetrekker.vercel.app is the visual source of truth:
editorial type hierarchy, restrained warm surfaces, a signature red accent, and
poster-first content. Android adds native feedback, safe system-bar treatment,
and touch-first ergonomics without changing the product identity.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize the title, artwork, and the next useful action.
- Use one red accent with calm, high-contrast neutral surfaces.
- Keep motion brief and explanatory; always honour reduced-motion preferences.
- Make loading, failure, and empty states informative and recoverable.
- Preserve enough space for text scaling and Android system UI.
